# Novar Whitepaper

## Vision

A Solana that feels instant: transactions that land in a fraction of a second, without asking builders to rewrite what already works.

## Mission

Deliver a faster lane for existing programs, keep authorship of every slot shared so no single seat controls it, and publish everything needed to verify the claims.

## How it works

Each slot is written by a trio of leaders. Batches are locked, cross-checked, opened together and sequenced into one order before stake-weighted votes finalize groups of slots.
