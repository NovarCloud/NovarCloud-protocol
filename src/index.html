The lane

Six beats to settle each slot.

Novar splits the labour for each slot among a trio of leaders, so a lone seat cannot stall progress or peek ahead. Here is the route each transaction travels.

  1. 01IntakeEvery leader collects a private batch of fresh transactions.
  2. 02LockBatches get wrapped, then a fingerprint is shared with the other two.
  3. 03Cross-checkFingerprints are compared; a silent leader is skipped, never waited on.
  4. 04UnwrapWhen the group is settled, all batches open together.
  5. 05SequenceEverything lines up in a single queue, ranked by the tip senders attach.
  6. 06ConfirmStake-weighted votes close each group of slots for good.
